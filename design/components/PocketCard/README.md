# PocketCard

A pocket: a place where money sits. Provide its name, its kind (Bank account, Cash, Fund, Broker), its currency, its balance and where the figure came from.

- The balance is in the pocket's own currency. When that is not the base currency, the converted value sits under it.
- A broker or fund also says what was paid, so paid and worth can be compared.
- The last line says whether the balance is synced or entered by hand.
