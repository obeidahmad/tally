# PocketsScreen

The Pockets page: the top bar ("Transfer" outline, "Add pocket" primary), a net worth card with the AreaChart and its 3M, 6M, 12M, 24M range, then AccountGroups on the left and the SummaryCard on the right (the summary on top under 1100px), and the table "Where your money should be": per pocket (bank, cash, fund and broker pockets) a bold row with its emoji and name, what it should hold, what it holds and the difference (holds minus should; short in `ty-num--neg`, nothing to move as a muted "–"), then a `ty-plan__sub` row per subsection kept there with its section dot, name and signed available under Should hold (overspending in `ty-num--neg`), and under the next-month pocket a row "Next month's money" (the left over and the sections' rests). On phones the subsection rows are left out and the three figures stay as columns.
