# SectionGroup

A section with its subsections: the grouped budget table. Provide the section's name, its colour slot, what it received and from which rule, and its rows.

- The header carries the section dot (`sec-1` to `sec-8`, in the order sections were created), the name, the rest so far as a neutral Pill, and the income rule in words.
- Header totals sit above the same columns as the rows.
- Rest is what the section received minus what its subsections claim.
- A tax-style section says "taken first" in its rule.
