# Pill

A short state label. Provide the text and one state.

- `ty-pill--positive`: money still available. Shows the plain amount.
- `ty-pill--warning`: less than 10% left. The text ends in "left". Also a section's rest below zero: "Rest −30.00".
- `ty-pill--negative`: overspent. The amount carries a minus sign and the word "over".
- No modifier: neutral facts such as a currency code, "Rest 75.00" or a goal's progress.

The colour never stands alone: the sign or the word says the same thing.
