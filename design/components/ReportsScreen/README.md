# ReportsScreen

The Reports page on its Cash flow tab: the top bar (title, tabs Cash flow, Spending and Net worth, the month stepper), four StatCards for the chosen month (Total income, Total spending, Net, Savings rate), the FlowDiagram card, then the "Cash flow" bars card with its 3, 6, 12 and 24 months control. The Spending tab is a DonutChart card, the stacked bars over time and Top payees; the Net worth tab is the AreaChart at full width.
